GameNightly

Find a night when your whole group is free, then put game night on everyone's calendar.

How it works

  1. 1. Start a game nightRun /gamenight in Discord, or create a session on the web, and invite your friends.
  2. 2. Find the overlapPlayers link Google Calendar (or mark their availability by hand), and GameNightly ranks the times when the most people are free.
  3. 3. Lock it inThe organizer picks a time, and GameNightly adds the event to their Google Calendar and invites the players.

Why we ask for Google Calendar access

Linking Google Calendar is optional. If you link it, GameNightly asks for the permissions below and uses each one only for what's listed.

PermissionWhat we use it for
See your free/busy times
calendar.events.freebusy		 To check when you're busy and suggest times when everyone is free. We only see busy and free blocks, never what your events are. We don't store them.
View and edit events on your calendars
calendar.events		 When you organize a game night and confirm a time, we create that one event on your primary calendar and invite the players. We also read your calendar's time zone so times show up correctly. We don't read, change or delete your other events.
See your email address
userinfo.email		 To know which Google account you linked, and so organizers can add you as a guest on the game night invite.

You can remove GameNightly's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See our Privacy Policy for what we store and for how long.
